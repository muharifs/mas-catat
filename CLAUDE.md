# Mas Catat (codename: Kang Admin)

Android app (React Native + Expo + TypeScript) for recording weekly member dues
and cash flow of a rotating-gathering group (arisan-style). Single user (the owner),
offline-first, no backend.

Expo/React Native rules live in AGENTS.md. Read it, and the matching Expo docs, before touching Expo or RN code.

## Language rules
- UI text: Indonesian.
- Code comments, identifiers, commit messages, docs in code: English.

## Domain rules (source of truth)
- A period has N members. The gathering rotates house to house, one host per week,
  until every member has hosted once, then the period ends.
- Host of a week can be changed on member request (until the week is closed, and not while that week's host payout stands: correct the payout first, owner decision 2026-11-10). A host change that swaps two weeks is rejected if either week has a standing payout. Changing only the meeting date (same host) stays allowed while the week is open, also after the payout: the date does not affect money (owner decision 2026-11-10). No member leaves mid-period; N is fixed.
- Correcting a member payment is rejected while that member, as host of a meeting in the payment's period, has a standing host payout (a corrected payout counts as unpaid): correct the host payout first, then the payment, then pay the host again (owner decision 2026-11-13, B3). Checked in domain (`src/domain/payment-correction.ts`) and repository.
- Skipping the last meeting (owner decision 2026-11-15, decision 33): only the last meeting (number = member_count) of the ACTIVE period can be skipped, while it is the oldest open meeting, all earlier meetings are closed with their hosts paid (a corrected payout counts as unpaid), and the meeting has no standing host payout. Not allowed for a period with fewer than 2 members. A skipped meeting carries no dues (no debt, offset or write-off), no host payout and no ledger entry, so actual kas never changes by skipping. The host already assigned to that week stays stored, but its host and date are locked. Dues already paid in advance for that week (allocations stay, but stop counting; host offsets and carry-over applications to that week too) become member credit ("Titipan") through the existing carry-over (may be a non-multiple of Rp15.000); a payment made while skipped never allocates to that week. Closing: the skipped week counts as done; the normal "Akhiri periode" closes the period and then the skipped week in the same transaction; remaining debt is still written off after the member's own credit settles arrears. Total dues of the period = 15.000 x N x (N - skipped weeks). The skip is undone with "Batalkan pelewatan" (reason required) only while the period is active; record = append-only `week_skips` plus `week_skip_undos` (migration 0006, view `skipped_weeks`); undo is rejected if it would make any member credit negative (e.g. the released credit was already refunded). The skipped member's turn needs no special storage: the owner picks them as host of meeting 1 of the next period. In the UI the status is "Dilewati".
- Cek kas / cash count (owner decision 2026-11-17, decision 34): per meeting the owner may count the physical Tunai (every meeting) and optionally Debit, and the app compares them with the ledger balances (`src/domain/cash-count.ts`, UI text "Cek kas"). A count is only a RECORD (append-only `cash_counts`, migration 0007: date, meeting, period, pos, expected, counted, difference = counted - expected, note): it never writes a ledger entry and never changes kas; differences are fixed with normal entries. Counted amounts are plain non-negative rupiah, NOT subject to the multiple-of-Rp15.000 rule. A wrong count is corrected by a NEW count; the latest per (meeting, pos) is effective (view `latest_cash_counts`), older ones stay in the history. Allowed only in the ACTIVE period, for the oldest open meeting or the latest closed one, never a skipped meeting; counts of a meeting that is later skipped are ignored in status and history. Expected balances are the global Tunai and Debit from the ledger, recomputed inside the write transaction. Closing a meeting without a count shows a warning only, never a block. Hints for a difference (member payment, host payout 10.000 x N, Tunai<->Debit transfer, shared-goal expense) are suggestions only. Cash counts are part of backup and restore (counted separately from transactions) and of the CSV export (fifth file "cek-kas", full history, status "Berlaku"/"Diganti").
- Removing a member: a member with no data at all (never in a period, no ledger, payment, loan or credit record) may be hard-deleted after confirmation. A member with any history is archived instead, never deleted: hidden from the member list and from new-period selection, history and reports stay intact, and archiving can be undone. Archiving is rejected while the member is in the active period, has a remaining loan, or holds credit (repay or refund first). Checked in domain and repository.
- The host is picked per meeting, not at period creation: only the current (oldest open) and the next week can be given a host, each member exactly once, and a week cannot be closed without a host. The meeting date is set together with the host. Meetings are on Tuesdays (a constant in `src/domain/schedule.ts`): the default date is the Tuesday closest to 7 days after the previous meeting (after a Wednesday +6 days, after a Monday +8, after a Saturday +10); later meetings keep estimated dates the same way; the first meeting defaults to the nearest Tuesday on or after today; chosen dates stay editable and stored dates are never rewritten. The period start date follows meeting 1's date while no meeting of the period is closed (changing meeting 1's date updates it); after a meeting is closed it stays fixed. The new-period form warns (no block) when meeting 1 is more than 14 days after today (owner decision 2026-11-12, F4). Breaks (e.g. Ramadan) carry no dues: the week simply stays open. In the UI a "week" is shown as "Pertemuan".
- Payments are separate records (member, date, amount), may be advance or late, and are allocated to weekly dues. Member payments always go into Tunai (money reaches Debit via a Kas transfer). Every payment MUST be a positive multiple of Rp15.000 (e.g. absent 2 weeks pays 30.000); partial payments are rejected. A payment above the remaining dues of the period is accepted and the excess is member credit, carried over to the member's next period as advance dues. The payment form warns before saving when part or all of a payment becomes credit. Credit may be a non-multiple of Rp15.000 (e.g. after a capped host offset), so carry-over can settle part of a week. If the member does not join the next period, the credit is refunded with one append-only cash-out entry (type `credit_refund`, from Tunai or Debit, the whole remaining credit). In the UI, member credit is called "Titipan" (code identifiers stay credit).
- Lump-sum payments are allocated to the oldest unpaid week first; each payment keeps its own date/week so the UI can show when it was actually paid.
- Host payout offset covers the host's unpaid weeks through the current meeting (oldest open meeting, or the last when all are closed; oldest weeks first), capped at the payout (10.000 x N); any debt beyond the cap stays as debt. Recorded as a separate entry.
- Every meeting (week), EVERY member (including that meeting's host) owes Rp15.000 (except a skipped meeting, see the skipping rule above).
  - Total collected = 15.000 x N.
  - Host receives 10.000 x N.
  - Group cash (kas) increases by 5.000 x N.
- If a member has not paid, the unpaid amount stays as a debt entry. When that member
  is the host, the debt is offset against the payout. Record it as TWO ledger entries
  (payout and offset), never as one silently reduced number, so reports stay auditable.
- At period close, unpaid member debt is written off and absorbed by kas: one append-only write-off entry per member (type `debt_writeoff`, no carry-over). Before writing off, the member's own credit settles that member's arrears (oldest first). Closing requires every meeting closed or skipped and every host paid (a corrected payout counts as unpaid). Reports distinguish actual kas (Tunai+Debit) from expected kas (actual + outstanding dues - member credit held). Credit money enters Tunai/Debit when received and is shown as a separate liability line ("Titipan anggota").
- Kas is split into pos: Tunai (cash), Debit (bank/e-wallet), Piutang (lent to members). Cash out also covers shared goals (e.g. tour).
- Transfers between Tunai and Debit go in either direction (one transfer entry). Piutang has no interest or deadline; repayments go into Tunai (not subject to the multiple-of-Rp15.000 rule). Shared-goal spending is a plain cash-out entry with a note. With no active period, kas entries go to the latest closed period (a loan repayment or a named old loan to the active period if the member is in it, otherwise the latest period containing the member; new loans only for members of the active period; with no period at all, reject).
- Loans are tied to members: remaining loan = lent - repaid over ALL periods after corrections (a reversal counts by its original's type). Only a member with remaining > 0 can repay, any positive amount up to the remaining (partial allowed), checked in domain and repository inside the write transaction (`src/domain/loans.ts`). An opening balance in Piutang MUST name the member and counts as that member's old loan; in Tunai/Debit it has no member. Reversing a loan or a named old loan is rejected when the member's remaining would go negative (correct the repayment first); reversing a repayment is always allowed. Old unnamed Piutang opening balances and old repayments above the loan are never migrated: they show in the Pinjaman list ("Saldo awal tanpa nama", "Kelebihan bayar"), cannot be repaid, and do not block backup or restore. Sum of member remaining + unnamed opening = Piutang balance. In the UI loans are "Pinjaman", screens `loans/`.
- Ledger is append-only. Corrections of closed weeks are reversal + new entry with a change trail; never overwrite. Append-only applies to normal use, not to a restore from a backup or to "Mulai dari nol": those replace ALL data on purpose (a verified safety copy is made first). One phone is active at a time (to move phones: backup, restore on the new phone, stop using the old one); data is never merged, ever.
- App name: Mas Catat, icon: small book. Tested on the owner's phone via Expo Go (no APK yet).
- Ledger: cash in / cash out entries, each with date, amount, type, member, week, note. The week is NULL on member_payment entries (one payment can span weeks; the detail lives in allocations).
- Money is stored as integer rupiah. Never use floating point.
- CSV ledger export (owner decision 2026-11-12, D10): an entry's amount goes in "Masuk" when it raises Tunai+Debit, in "Keluar" when it lowers Tunai+Debit, and in "Tanpa perubahan kas" when Tunai+Debit does not change (Tunai<->Debit transfer, debt write-off, Piutang opening balance). Exactly one amount column per row; sum Masuk - Keluar = actual kas (Tunai+Debit). Loans go to Keluar, loan repayments to Masuk. Rule lives in `csvAmountColumns` (`src/domain/flow.ts`).

## Technical decisions
- Expo (managed) + TypeScript strict. Navigation: expo-router.
- Local storage: expo-sqlite. Plain SQL migrations, versioned.
- Business rules (dues, payout, kas, offset) live in pure functions in `src/domain/`
  with unit tests. UI never computes money itself.
- Phase 2: export report to Google Sheets (CSV first, API later). Public read-only report.
- Do not add a backend unless the owner asks.
